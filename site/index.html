Meet Zoya

A friendly AI helper that works in your browser. Ask her to search, read, fill in and click through websites, by typing or by talking, in English or Urdu. She always asks before anything important.

Download Zoya

What she does

Works in her own tab

Zoya uses a tab group called “Zoya”, so your other tabs stay yours.

Asks before risky steps

Submitting, sending, buying or deleting always needs your OK. Things that can't be undone need a click.

Talks with you

Hands-free voice in English or Urdu. Say “stop” or “رکو” any time.

Learns skills

Save a task that worked, test it, approve it, and she'll do it faster next time.

Your choice of AI

Google Gemini (free tier), OpenAI, Claude, other compatible providers, or private local Ollama.

Your data stays yours

Your key and settings stay in your Chrome. Shared knowledge packs never include keys, passwords or logins.

Install in 5 minutes

  1. Download the latest ZIP from the button above and extract it to a folder you'll keep (for example Documents\Zoya).
  2. In Chrome, open chrome://extensions and switch on Developer mode (top right).
  3. Click Load unpacked and choose the extracted folder.
  4. Click the puzzle-piece icon in Chrome's toolbar and pin “NexForce with Zoya”. Click it to open Zoya.
  5. Get a free key at Google AI Studio (Get API key → Create API key, no card needed), and paste it into Zoya's setup.

Keep the folder where it is. If you delete or move it, Chrome removes the extension. To update, replace the folder's contents with the new version and click the reload icon on chrome://extensions.

Good to know

Cloud AI

With a cloud provider, the page text Zoya reads and what you ask are sent to that company. Use local Ollama, or do it yourself, for banking and private accounts.

Voice

Listening uses Chrome's speech service (Google). Chrome has no Urdu voice, so Urdu replies are shown as text. Microsoft Edge has a free Urdu voice, and Zoya works there too.

Free limits

Free AI tiers have daily limits. Zoya tells you if you hit one, and can switch to a backup provider.
